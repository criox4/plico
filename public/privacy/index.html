Privacy Policy

Effective 27 September 2026 · Plico is run by Ashutosh Pradhan, an individual in India (“we”, “us”). Contact: privacy@plico.space

The short version. Plico keeps track of shared expenses between you and people you choose. We collect what that needs and nothing more. No ads, no tracking across apps or websites, no selling or renting of data, no access to your bank, SMS or contacts. Money never passes through Plico: payments happen in your own UPI app. You can download or delete your data at any time.

1. Who is responsible

Ashutosh Pradhan is the Data Fiduciary under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”) and the data controller under the EU and UK GDPR for personal data processed through Plico (the app at plico.space and the Plico apps for Android and iOS). Plico’s source code is public at github.com/criox4; this policy covers the service we run at plico.space, not copies others run.

Grievance Officer (DPDP Act and IT Rules, 2021): Ashutosh Pradhan, privacy@plico.space. We acknowledge grievances within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 15 days, and in any case within the time the law allows.

2. What we collect

You give us

  • Account: your name, email address and password (stored only as a salted hash), or your Google account’s name, email and profile photo if you sign in with Google.
  • Age: whether you are 18 or older or 13 to 17. For 13 to 17 year olds, your parent’s or guardian’s email, the name they give, and when they consented.
  • Profile (optional): phone number, UPI ID, profile picture (a photo, an emoji or a generated Plico face), app theme and reminder tone.
  • Groups and expenses: group names, emoji and cover photos; expense titles, amounts, dates, categories, who paid and who owes; settlements and whether they were confirmed; receipt photos you attach.
  • People you add: the names you give them and, if you choose, their email address, phone number or UPI ID, so they can be invited, reminded and paid. Only add details you are allowed to share.

Collected automatically

  • Sign-in sessions: IP address, device and browser type (user agent), and when each session started and expires. We use these to keep your account secure and to show you the Devices page.
  • Technical logs: our hosting keeps short-lived request logs (such as IP address, time and error details) to run and protect the service.

We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, cookies for tracking, fingerprinting, location, contacts, SMS, microphone or bank data. The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to attach or scan a photo.

4. Who else sees it

  • People in your groups see the group, its members, expenses, settlements, receipt photos and the profile details needed to split and pay (name, profile picture, UPI ID). Anyone you send a pay link to sees the names, amount and UPI ID in that link.
  • Service providers that process data for us, under contracts that limit them to our instructions:
    • Supabase: database and file storage (Mumbai, India region).
    • Vercel: hosting of the website and API (global infrastructure; requests may be processed outside India).
    • Resend (United States) and Zoho ZeptoMail (India): sending emails.
    • Google: only if you choose “Continue with Google”, to confirm who you are.
    • OpenRouter (United States) routing to OpenAI: only if you turn on AI reading. We ask OpenRouter to use only providers that do not store or train on your data.
  • Apps you choose: when you pay, Plico hands the payee’s UPI ID and the amount to your UPI app; when you send a reminder or invite, your WhatsApp or email app sends it. Their own privacy policies apply there.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

Transfers outside India: some providers above process data outside India. We only use countries not restricted by the Government of India under the DPDP Act, and for EU/UK users we rely on the providers’ standard contractual clauses.

5. How long we keep it

  • Your account and profile: until you delete your account.
  • Expenses in shared groups: they stay for the other members after you leave or delete your account, with your name shown as a guest, because they are also the other members’ records. You can ask us to remove your name from them.
  • Sign-in sessions: until they expire (7 days after last use) or you sign out.
  • Verification, reset and consent links: until used or expired (1 hour for sign-in links, 14 days for a parent’s consent link).
  • If a parent declines consent, the child’s account is deleted immediately.
  • Backups: deleted data leaves our backups within 30 days.
  • Email providers and hosting logs keep delivery and request logs for their own short, stated periods.

6. Your rights

You can, at any time and free of charge:

  • Access and download your data: Account → Privacy and data → Download my data.
  • Correct it: edit your profile, groups and expenses in the app.
  • Erase it: Account → Delete account, or see how to delete your account.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off AI reading in Privacy and data, or delete your account. Withdrawal doesn’t affect what was done before.
  • Nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or become incapable (DPDP s.14): email us.
  • Grievance redressal: write to our Grievance Officer at privacy@plico.space. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
  • In the EU and UK you also have the rights to data portability, restriction and objection, and to complain to your data protection authority.

We answer requests within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm it’s your account first.

7. Children

Plico is not for children under 13, and they cannot create an account. People aged 13 to 17 can use Plico only after a parent or lawful guardian gives consent through a link we email them, confirming they are the parent or guardian and 18 or older. We do not track, monitor the behaviour of, or target advertising at anyone, including children. A parent can withdraw consent at any time by writing to privacy@plico.space; the account is then deleted. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, tell us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed. Photos are private to their group and are only served through our API after checking membership; profile pictures are served at unguessable addresses. Access to production systems is limited to the operator. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

9. On your device

The app keeps a copy of your groups and any unsynced changes on your device so it works offline, plus your sign-in (a secure cookie on the web, a token in the app on Android and iOS). The home-screen widget shows a summary of your balance. See Cookies and storage. Signing out removes these.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app or by email before it applies. The effective date above shows the current version.
