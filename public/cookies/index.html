Cookies and storage

Effective 27 September 2026

Plico uses no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. It only stores what it needs to sign you in and to work offline, so there is nothing to opt out of and no cookie banner.

What we store

NameWhereWhyHow long
better-auth.session_tokenCookie (plico.space, secure, httpOnly)Keeps you signed in on the web7 days, renewed while you use Plico; removed when you sign out
better-auth.state and similarCookie, during Google sign-in on the web onlyProtects the Google sign-in stepMinutes
splittr, splittr-outboxLocal storage on your deviceA copy of your groups and changes not yet synced, so Plico works offlineUntil you sign out or clear site data
splittr-tokenApp storage (Android and iOS apps only)Keeps you signed in in the appUntil you sign out
App filesService worker cacheLets Plico open without a connectionReplaced on each update
Widget summaryApp storage (Android and iOS apps only)Shows your balance on the home-screen widgetUntil you sign out

These are strictly necessary for a service you asked for, so they don’t need consent under Indian, EU or UK law. You can clear them in your browser’s site settings; you’ll be signed out and offline data not yet synced will be lost.
