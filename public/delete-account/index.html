Delete your Plico account

Plico, by Ashutosh Pradhan

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Plico and sign in.
  2. Tap You, then Delete account.
  3. Confirm with the link we email you. Your account is deleted as soon as you open it.

Without the app

Email privacy@plico.space from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We confirm it’s you, delete it within 7 days, and reply when it’s done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: name, email, password, profile picture, phone, UPI ID, age and consent records.
  • Your sign-in sessions and devices.
  • Groups you are the only member of, with their expenses and photos.

What stays, and why

  • Expenses and settlements in groups shared with other people stay for them, because they are their records too. Your name appears there as a guest, without your account, email or photo. Ask us if you want your name removed as well.
  • Deleted data leaves our backups within 30 days.

Before deleting, you can download everything: You → Privacy and data → Download my data.
